date: 2026-07-28

authors: [makisuo]
---

Traces, Logs, Metrics and Replays now sit together under one **Explore** section in the sidebar,
the same way Infrastructure groups its pages. URLs such as `/traces` and `/logs` are unchanged, so
your bookmarks and shared links still work.

Also in Explore:

- **One color per service.** A service's color comes from its name, so it matches across charts,
  traces and the service map, and it no longer shifts when you change the time range.
- **Trace timeline.** Zoom and pan stay inside the trace, hover is much faster, and you can zoom
  with W/S, pan with A/D, and press F to fit.
- **Duration filters.** Traces offers one-click > p50, > p95 and > 1s presets from live stats.
  Replays adds a session-length histogram you can drag to pick a range.
- **Latency colors.** p50, p95 and p99 values are colored by how slow they are.
- **Traces** works at every screen size, the advanced filter editor has syntax highlighting, and
  **Replays** open in a player-first layout.
- **Service map.** One node per database, Hyperdrive collapsed into a single node, an environment
  switcher, Cloudflare edge analytics on instrumented Workers, and no layout shift while loading.
- **Metrics** opens as a browsable grid with a shareable page per metric.

Read more in the [traces guide](/docs/explore/traces).
