Dashboards used to be one flat canvas, so a board with thirty widgets was one long scroll. You can
now organize widgets into **groups** that collapse, and give a group tabs when it holds more than
one view.

- **Add a group** from the toolbar, or with the `+` on any group. Double-click a group name to
  rename it, reorder groups with move up and move down, and mark a group **Collapsed by default**.
- **Move a widget** between groups from its menu with **Move to…**.
- **Delete safely.** Deleting a group asks whether to keep its widgets or delete them. Deleting a
  tab asks which tab should receive its widgets, and names that tab in the button.
- **Your view is yours.** Which groups are collapsed and which tab is open live in the URL, so
  collapsing a group changes nothing for the next person who opens the board, and you can share a
  link that opens on a specific tab.
