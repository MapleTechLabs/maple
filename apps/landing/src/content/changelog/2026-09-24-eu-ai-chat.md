---
title: "AI chat is available in the EU region"
description: "EU organizations can use Maple's AI chat, with every model request served by providers inside the EU."
date: 2026-09-24
category: ai
authors: [makisuo]
---

The EU region launched with AI chat turned off, because a chat turn sends your spans and logs to a
model provider. It's on now for EU organizations.

AI requests from an EU organization are served only by model providers inside the EU. If a model
has no EU provider, the request fails rather than being routed to the US.

Open chat anywhere in the EU dashboard with `C`, or from the command palette. The same routing
applies to investigations and issue triage for EU organizations.

Read more about the [EU region](/eu).
